## Specific notes

1. **Orient is under-grounded, and this should be stated in the Skill.** It should keep Orient short and grounded: find a starting point, record the baseline, list must-not-break behavior. It should not import a full requirements-engineering or codebase-archaeology method.
2. **Commit's stop condition and Close's retrospective are DERIVED glue.** Label them as such in the Skill.
3. **Solo-developer adaptation runs through all stages.** Every Shape Up and Wake source assumes a team, customer or shaper. EBS assumes a team schedule. Applying them to one developer's inner loop is an adaptation throughout. The extraction files flag this under *Source limitations*.
4. **The optional Shape Up chapters were necessary.** Without *Hand Over Responsibility* there would be almost nothing for Orient. Without *Show Progress* and *Decide When to Stop*, De-risk and Close would lack their uphill/downhill and baseline rules.
5. **Not fetched:** Wake's PDF summary sheet for *Twenty Ways* (linked from the page) and other Wake INVEST sub-articles (Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable). The *Estimable* and *Small* articles would likely strengthen Estimate/Slice and are public.
